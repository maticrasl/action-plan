# Help and reports

- **How do I…?** Open the Guide in the app.
- **Something is broken, or you miss something:** in the app, Settings › Help › *Report a problem*
  or *Suggest a feature* (also at the end of the Guide). They open this repository's issue forms.

Reports here are public. Action Plan keeps your plans in your browser and, if you sync, in your
own Google Drive: its makers cannot see them. So describe what you did and what happened, and
leave out anything private from your plans.
