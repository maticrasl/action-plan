# Tomaž Hribar – the carpenter with a loan

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Age, place | 45, a village near Kranj |
| Work | Owns a joinery workshop with two employees: kitchens, stairs, built-in furniture |
| Home | Wife Petra (teacher), sons aged 14 and 11 |
| Language | Slovenian |
| Devices | Dusty Windows laptop in the workshop office (Chrome), Android phone with a cracked screen |
| Account | Google, on both devices |
| Confidence with technology | Uses invoicing software and online banking; no patience for anything fiddly |
| Opens the app | Sunday evening for twenty minutes on the laptop; the phone once or twice mid-week |

## Story

Two years ago Tomaž took a loan for a CNC machine. The work is there, the money is always
late: customers pay in sixty days, suppliers want theirs in fifteen. He works six days and does
the paperwork on the seventh. His accountant told him to keep a reserve; his wife told him the
boys are growing up without him.

He does not like apps. His older son installed this one and said, “Just try it, ati.” He stayed
because it showed him one number going down.

## What he wants

- »Da enkrat neham gasit požare.«
- The loan gone in four years, not six.
- Three months of costs in reserve.
- Sundays without the workshop. He does not believe this one.

## His plan

Two plans: **Delavnica** and **Doma**.

Vision: none at first. In month 3 Petra, looking over his shoulder, makes him add
**Podjetje, ki dela zame, ne jaz zanj**.

## How he uses the app

- Sunday: bank statement on one side, the app on the other. Enters three numbers, ticks the
  week, looks at the loan graph, closes.
- Types amounts the way he writes them: `38.400`, `1.250,50`, sometimes `500 €`.
- Titles are terse, noun and verb, no full stop.
- On the phone only to tick “Izstavi račune” on Friday from the van, with thick fingers and
  sawdust on the screen.
- Never opens the Map. Likes the Timeline for the offers, because it looks like his wall
  planner.
- Asked his son to “print it” for the bank adviser.

## Where it goes wrong

- `38.400` typed into a number field: the Slovenian thousands dot may be read as a decimal
  point and give 38,4. He would not notice until the graph looked odd.
- Months with nothing put aside: he enters `0`, or nothing. The per-month goal should read the
  same either way.
- Laptop offline in the workshop for days (the router is in the house); phone changes and laptop
  changes meet on Sunday.
- The laptop clock is wrong by an hour since the last time change.
- “Nedelja prosta” on a set day: he ticks it on Sunday evening from the workshop office.
- A customer pays late, so “Rezerva” goes backwards in his head; a state that adds up cannot go
  down unless he enters a negative amount. He tries `-2000`.
- Google asks for confirmation about once an hour, so on Sundays the first thing he meets is
  “sync is paused”.

## How he reacts

Practical and blunt. A wrong number makes him distrust the whole thing at once. A line going
down on the loan graph is the only reward he wants. Friendly texts about feelings he skips;
he would read one that told him what to do next. He will not ask for help; he will stop using
it.

## Over time

Reliable on Sundays for months, apart from July and August (season, no Sundays at all). The
Doma plan withers and is the one that matters. After half a year he either merges it into
Delavnica “so it's all in one place” or deletes it.

## Voice

- »Koliko še. To me zanima.«
- »Ne rabim, da me aplikacija hvali.«
- »Zakaj mi kaže 38, če je 38 tisoč?«

## Good for testing

- Amounts with thousands separators, currency signs, negative amounts, zero.
- One state serving two goals, in total and per month; months with no entries.
- States measured monthly; graphs with few points over a long time.
- Custom repeat by day of the month; set weekday; times per week.
- Lists and dependencies for offers; Timeline.
- Two devices, long offline stretches, a clock that is off; paused sync on every visit.
- Two plans, merge of plans.
- Slovenian with € and business words; printing or sharing a view.
