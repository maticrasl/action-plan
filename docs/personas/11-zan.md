# Žan Kralj – one goal, no reading

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Age, place | 24, Celje |
| Work | Forklift driver in a distribution warehouse; mornings one week, afternoons the next |
| Home | With his parents, in his childhood room |
| Language | Slovenian interface; types without č, š, ž, in dialect, with typos |
| Devices | A cheap Android phone, small screen (360 px wide), Chrome, 40 tabs open, storage always full |
| Account | Guest. Sees “Log in with Google” and thinks it wants something from him |
| Confidence with technology | Uses the phone all day, for games and video. Has never filled in a form by choice |
| Opens the app | Twice in the first week, then when something reminds him: payday, an argument at home |

## Story

Žan wants to move out. For that he needs a car to take a better job in Žalec, and for the car
he needs money, and the money goes: energy drinks, betting slips, a phone on instalments. His
girlfriend Tjaša sent him a link with “probi to”. He opened it in the bus on the way home.

He is not lazy and not stupid. He has never been asked what he wants in a way that needed an
answer in writing, and the app asked him five times in one form.

## What he wants

- »avto. pol pa stran od doma.«
- 6.000 € for a used Golf.
- He would not call the rest goals: fewer energy drinks, the gym he pays for and does not go
  to, no betting. Tjaša would.

## His plan

One plan, with the name the app gave it. He never renamed it.

No visions.

| Kind | Title | Details |
|---|---|---|
| Goal | prihranit 6000 za avto | Measurement, from 0 to 6000, deadline: he picked the last day the date picker opened on, then changed it to “next summer” |
| State | prisparano | Replaces. He did not understand “Adds up” and left it off, so he enters the total from his banking app: 150, 400, 380, 700 |
| Routine | fitnes | 3× per week. Ticked four times in all |
| Activity | prodat stari bajk | One-time, no date |
| Activity | vprasat seva za visjo placo | One-time, no date. Will not be done |

That is all. Category, description, details, links: empty. The routine is linked to nothing.

## How he uses the app

- Thumb only, in portrait, on the bus or in the break, with a video playing in another tab.
- Skipped the tour at the first screen. Closes anything that covers the screen without reading.
- Types the least a field accepts: one word, a full stop, “ne vem”, “ker”. No capital letters,
  no diacritics, many typos (“prihrnait”).
- Taps the same thing three times when nothing seems to happen.
- Presses the phone's Back button to leave a form, a dialog, a view, the app.
- Opens the app from the link in the chat with Tjaša each time; never installed it, has no
  bookmark.

## Where it goes wrong

- The goal form. “Kaj točno?”, “Zakaj je to uresničljivo?”, “Zakaj je pomembno?”: he typed “.”
  and “ne vem”, and whether the form let him save decides whether he ever saw the rest of the
  app.
- He did not link the state to the goal. Money goes in, the goal stays at 0%. He concluded it
  does not work.
- The savings went down (400 → 380). A goal that goes backwards with no comment.
- A date field: he does not know when. “Next summer” is as exact as it gets.
- Back button behaviour inside dialogs and forms; half-filled forms lost.
- Double and triple taps on Add and Save: two goals with the same name.
- A full phone: the browser dropped the site's data after three weeks away, or he cleared it
  himself. Guest, no backup, everything gone. He shrugged and did not start again for a month.
- Words he does not use: stanje, dejavnost, rutina, vizija. “Trenutno stanje” means his bank
  account to him, which this once is right.
- Small screen: long labels, the keyboard covering the field he types in, the Save button under
  it.

## How he reacts

He gives a thing fifteen seconds. If the first screen asks a question he cannot answer, he
leaves, and not angrily; he just has other tabs. What holds him is a number that is his: the
day the bar showed 12% he sent Tjaša a screenshot. Being told off he knows from home and from
work and does not need from a phone. Long kind texts are still long.

## Over time

Day 1: one goal, with a struggle. Day 3: adds the state after Tjaša asks. Payday: enters money.
Then three weeks of nothing. Then data loss or a red goal. If he gets through that, he becomes
a once-a-month user with one goal and one number, and reaches 6.000 € four months later than
“next summer”. For him that is the app working.

## Voice

- »kaj naj tle napisem«
- »sam cifro bi vpisal«
- »a se mi je vse zbrisal? ok.«

## Good for testing

- The very first minutes: empty app, tour skipped, first goal, how little is needed.
- Goal form with minimal and meaningless answers; missing deadline.
- An unlinked state and goal: does the app say why nothing moves.
- Smallest phone width; keyboard over fields; long Slovenian labels; thumb reach.
- Back button, double taps, duplicates.
- Slovenian without diacritics and with typos; search or filters that should still match.
- Guest data loss without backup; the warning about plans kept in this browser only.
- One goal for a year; a goal that goes backwards; monthly use.
- Opening from a shared link inside a chat app's browser.
