# Jože Zupančič – the careful one

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Age, place | 68, Novo mesto |
| Work | Retired mechanical engineer, 38 years in one factory |
| Home | Wife Marija; daughter Mateja lives in Ljubljana and visits every second weekend |
| Language | Slovenian only |
| Devices | iPad (Safari), text size set large in the system, reading glasses. A button phone |
| Account | Guest. Mateja logs him in with his Google account in week 5 |
| Confidence with technology | Low with screens, high with numbers and procedures |
| Opens the app | Every morning at the kitchen table after measuring, for ten unhurried minutes |

## Story

Jože had a heart attack in spring. The cardiologist told him: lose weight, walk every day,
write down your blood pressure, come back in six months. He wrote it in a squared notebook for
two months, in his draughtsman's hand. Mateja set up the app on the iPad so that he could see
the line go down, and built the first plan with him from a template.

He is precise and patient. He reads every word on the screen, top to bottom, before touching
anything. He is afraid of pressing something that cannot be undone.

## What he wants

- »Da bom še videl vnuka, ko bo šel v šolo.«
- To bring the numbers to the doctor and have the doctor nod.
- To do it properly. If there is a correct way to fill something in, he wants to know it.

## His plan

One plan: **Zdravje**, started from a template and changed.

Vision: **Biti zdrav in pri močeh za družino** (Mateja added it; he would not have)

| Kind | Title | Details |
|---|---|---|
| Goal | Shujšati na 88 kg do kontrole pri kardiologu | Measurement, from 96,4 to 88, deadline in five months |
| Goal | Krvni tlak pod 135 | Measurement, from 152 to 135, same deadline |
| Goal | Vsak dan hoditi 30 minut | Routines kept |
| State | Teža (kg) | Replaces. Measured every morning, one decimal, with a comma |
| State | Krvni tlak – zgornji (mmHg) | Replaces. Daily |
| State | Krvni tlak – spodnji (mmHg) | Replaces. Daily. Linked to no goal |
| State | Prehojeni kilometri | Adds up. Entered after each walk: 2,4 · 3,1 · 2,8 |
| Routine | Hoja 30 minut | Every day. Improves the kilometres state, so the tick asks how much |
| Routine list | Jutranja zdravila | Daily. Items: Aspirin, Zaviralec beta, Statin (zvečer!), Tlak izmerjen |
| Routine | Kontrola teže pri zdravnici | Custom: every 4 weeks |
| Activity | Naroči se na kontrolo pri kardiologu | One-time, done |
| List | Vprašanja za kardiologa | Items added over weeks |

Each state has its description filled in exactly: »Zjutraj, po stranišču, pred zajtrkom, brez
copat.«

## How he uses the app

- Same order every day: medicines list, weight, pressure (two values), then the walk in the
  afternoon, kilometres from the car's trip counter along his route.
- Types slowly with one finger. Proper sentences, capital letters, full stops, units.
- Zooms in with two fingers and then cannot find his way back; turns the iPad sideways for
  graphs.
- Looks at the graphs every Sunday and copies the weekly averages into the notebook, which he
  has not given up.
- Never long-presses anything by intent. Finds menus by accident and closes them at once.

## Where it goes wrong

- Blood pressure is two numbers. He tried to type `135/85` into one field. Two states was
  Mateja's answer; he finds it clumsy.
- He once typed the weight into the pressure state. He did not know how to remove it and
  waited two weeks for Mateja.
- He measures twice on a bad morning and wants both values kept; the second replaces the first.
- A dialog closing because he touched beside it makes him think he lost what he typed.
- “Statin (zvečer!)” sits in a morning list, so the day's tick waits until evening.
- English or technical words in the interface (sync, export) stop him. He asks Marija.
- He does not know where the data is. Before week 5 it was only on the iPad, and he had asked
  twice whether “it is saved”.

## How he reacts

A week where the weight goes up worries him more than it should; he measures again. He takes
late marks as a reprimand and fixes them the same day. Praise from the app embarrasses him a
little. Clear instructions calm him; anything that moves by itself unsettles him.

## Over time

Steady as a clock for months, the opposite of most users. By month 4 he has 120 measurements
per state. A hospital stay of six days in month 3 leaves a gap he wants to fill in afterwards
from the hospital's paper, day by day.

## Voice

- »Kam naj to vpišem, da bo prav?«
- »A je to zdaj shranjeno? Kje se to vidi?«
- »Včeraj je kazalo 91,2, danes 91,8. Nekaj sem naredil narobe.«

## Good for testing

- Slovenian texts read word by word: anything unclear or untranslated is found.
- Decimal comma; daily measurements over months; deleting a wrong value; filling in past days.
- A routine that improves a state that adds up (tick asks “how much?”).
- Routine list, every day; custom repeat every four weeks.
- Tablet in both orientations, system text size large, browser zoom.
- Dialogs closing by accident; no undo.
- Template as a starting point; first Google login done by someone else on his device.
- Graphs with dense, long, regular data.
