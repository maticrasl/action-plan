# Ingrid Solheim – the long-time user

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Age, place | 58, Bergen |
| Work | Head teacher of a primary school; retires in fourteen months |
| Home | Husband Arne; two grown children; a cabin in the mountains with no signal |
| Language | English interface (her second language; fluent, formal) |
| Devices | School laptop (Edge, locked down), home desktop (Chrome), iPad on the sofa, iPhone |
| Account | Google, on all four |
| Confidence with technology | Competent and methodical. Reads release notes |
| Opens the app | Daily for five minutes; a real weekly review every Sunday at seven, without fail |

## Story

Ingrid has used the app for eighteen months, longer than anyone. It began with one goal, to
walk the Camino de Santiago the spring after she retires, and grew into the place where her
last working years and her first free ones are arranged. She is the user for whom the method
works as written: she measures, reviews, pauses, lets go.

Her plan is large because it is old, not because it is messy. She fears two things: that
something she has kept for a year and a half disappears, and that the app becomes slow.

## What she wants

- “To leave the school in good hands, and to leave it on my feet.”
- To walk 780 kilometres in May, at sixty, without injury.
- To speak enough Spanish to talk with people on the way.
- To know what their money allows after two salaries become two pensions.
- To have something to get up for on the first Monday.

## Her plan

Three plans: **Camino**, **School handover**, **After**.

Visions:

- **A useful and curious old age** (top; her Purpose, in gold for the past year)
  - To be strong enough to go where I want on my own legs
  - To leave things better than I found them
  - To keep learning
  - To be free of money worries, and generous

Totals after eighteen months: 31 goals (17 Achieved, 4 Paused, 10 Active), 14 states with about
1,900 measurements and amounts between them, some 260 activities (200 done), 22 routines of

## How she uses the app

- Weekdays: Overview on the phone at breakfast, tick, measure, close.
- Sunday review at the desktop: ticks, measures, graphs, then the Timeline to move what no
  longer holds. Pauses and resumes goals by season.
- At the cabin for a week at a time with the iPad, offline, entering walks; the phone gets
  signal on the ridge and syncs half a day's entries.
- Exports a full copy of each plan on the first of the month to a folder on the home desktop.
- Looks back often: last year's graph, an achieved goal's notes.
- Careful English, full sentences. Norwegian place names with å, ø, æ.

## Where it goes wrong

- Offline for a week on one device while another is in use: the same routine ticked on both,
  the same state measured on both, an item edited on one and deleted on the other.
- The school laptop blocks pop-ups and third-party sign-in; Google confirmation fails there
  and she sees “sync is paused” for the whole working day.
- The cabin iPad's clock drifted; “the later change wins” took the wrong one once.
- Scale: the Map with everything on it, the Timeline across eighteen months, a graph with 300
  amounts, the Activities board with 200 done cards before “Hide done and achieved”.
- Ended routines and achieved goals: where do they go, and do they still count somewhere?
- Space as thousands separator in `41 350`.
- Her Purpose lost its gold the day she added a goal in “After” without linking it. She
  noticed within the week.
- An update of the app while a tab had been open for days.

## How she reacts

Calm and exact. She reports problems in numbered steps and expects an answer. She forgives a
rough edge and does not forgive lost data. She likes that the app does not chatter. A change in
where something sits annoys her for a week, then she adapts; a change in what a number means
she would take as a breach of trust.

## Over time

For simulation her history is the point: build eighteen months of regular data, two long gaps
(a broken wrist in the first winter, three weeks; the school's inspection, two weeks), a season
pattern in the walking, and goals achieved every month or two. Going forward: retirement, the
School handover plan achieved whole and kept as a record, departure, six weeks of walking with
the phone only and little signal.

## Voice

- “This worked last week and does not today. Steps to reproduce follow.”
- “I do not need it to be clever. I need it to be the same tomorrow.”
- “Where can I see what I did a year ago?”

## Good for testing

- Old, large data: draw times of every view, storage size, sync size, export and import of big
  plans.
- Four devices; long offline stretches; conflicting edits, ticks and measurements; edit against
  delete; clocks that disagree.
- Sync blocked by a locked-down browser.
- Achieved and paused goals, ended routines, hiding done items.
- Visions tree with a Purpose kept over time, lost and regained.
- Routines with end dates; custom repeats by weekday of the month.
- Long lists; chains of dependencies; sub-goals.
- Graph zoom over long periods; states that add up with hundreds of amounts.
- App updates over stored data from many earlier versions.
- Letters å, ø, æ; numbers with a space as separator.
